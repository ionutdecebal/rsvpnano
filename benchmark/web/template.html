What counts as a pass?

Both font sizes must display all 1,000 words in order, with no skipped words and no measured reading cycle over 60 ms. Each session must finish within 120 ms of one minute. Missing, interrupted or malformed results cannot pass.

This tests the current Word-mode UI, built-in Literata, SD-backed words, phantom words, and a changing Focus badge. Extra pacing delays are zero. Update, drawing and display flush are timed on the Nano.

A pass covers these two sessions. It does not certify other fonts or scripts, chunk mode, the full app’s Wi-Fi/battery/persistence work, or physical panel scanout. The longest word gap is reported separately.

USB activity & troubleshooting

On Linux, macOS or Windows, use a current Chrome or Edge browser. Keep only this page connected to the Nano. If the two-minute connection window expires, press RESET, prepare the drive again, eject and reconnect. To repeat a test, start again at step 2.
