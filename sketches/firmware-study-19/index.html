About this proposal

This is an interactive HTML design study, not a firmware build or performance test. It adds an SD-card check before the reading lessons, keeping Study 18’s companion QR, Wi-Fi/USB upload guide and permanent Add books entry in Help.

The introduction practices on its own short sample. Speed and pacing remain draft choices until you press Use settings; Keep current continues without applying them. Completion or skipping is remembered in browser storage alongside interface language, tap/hold preference and your chosen speed and pacing. Simulate restart checks that behaviour; Replay introduction works without clearing the completion flag.

The Study 17 firmware already implements the saved introduction flag. This Study 19 addition is HTML-only: the SD-card onboarding and QR upload guide have not yet been added to firmware. The previous flashable file remains unchanged.

The proposed flows support English, German and Romanian; inherited device/connection pages are context. Other settings last for this page session. The speed exercise helps compare what feels comfortable; it does not measure comprehension or automatically determine an ideal WPM. The browser’s inherited word-length heuristic illustrates long/complex-word pauses and is not a firmware pacing benchmark. Browser fonts, simulated device actions and immediate hold-release pauses do not establish firmware memory use, touch performance or timing.
