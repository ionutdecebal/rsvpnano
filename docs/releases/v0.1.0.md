# v0.1.0

## Native watch orientation

- Watch-sized boards now use their native display axes: 368 x 448 on AMOLED
  1.8 v1/v2, 410 x 502 on AMOLED 2.06, 480 x 480 on AMOLED 2.16,
  450 x 600 on AMOLED 2.41, and 172 x 320 on LCD 1.47.
- Correct the landscape logical dimensions that caused cropped or mirrored
  output on AMOLED controllers without quarter-turn rotation support.
- Preserve panel offsets when flipping orientation for left-handed use.
- Fit the Read and Device progress rings, narrow-screen actions, text sizing,
  and appearance editor controls to the native watch dimensions.
- Continue drawing directly to the panel: no additional canvas or framebuffer.
  Boards still compile only their selected presentation; LCD 3.49 retains its
  landscape orientation and regular layout.

## Reader appearance

- Replace separate reader and typography settings pages with an interactive
  appearance editor using the reader's font, guides, and chrome rendering.
- Adjust font family, size, tracking, anchor, guide width, and guide gap. Tap
  preview words to toggle focus highlighting and phantom words.
- Configure battery icon, battery label, chapter, progress, and arrow visibility
  independently for Reading and Paused states. Companion settings expose the
  same visibility choices.
- Keep rotary gestures attached to the selected control and prevent appearance
  preview redraws from clearing neighboring controls.
- Show an uncluttered page preview during reading-position and paragraph drags.
- The previous visibility boolean keys are replaced by `always`, `reading`,
  `paused`, and `never` choices. Older visibility preferences revert to defaults;
  review them in the appearance editor after updating. Other settings are retained.

## Companion and navigation

- Bound upload scratch memory and close USB sessions after failed uploads.
- Refresh companion branding and website credits.
- Synchronize feed articles beyond the first five entries.
- Make chapter selection follow finger movement.

## Testing notes

Host tests cover regular and watch controls, appearance layouts, settings, and
native watch dimensions. Firmware builds cannot confirm physical panel startup
or touch behavior. Please report the exact board, hardware revision, and steps
to reproduce any remaining problem.

The separate AMOLED 2.41 cold-boot blank-screen report is not confirmed fixed by
this release. Select the correct 1.8 v1 or v2 firmware for your hardware.
